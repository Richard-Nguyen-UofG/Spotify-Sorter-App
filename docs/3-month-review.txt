SPOTIFY SORTER: 3-MONTH REVIEW PLAN
===================================
Real use started: 2026-10-19
Review due:       around 2027-01-19 (after ~12 weekly sorts)

Goal: learn from real use where suggestions, Explore and the models help or fall short,
and decide what to change. Everything below comes from what the app logs while you use it;
nothing needs to be filled in by hand.


WHERE THE DATA IS
-----------------
App database:   %APPDATA%\com.richardnguyen.spotifysorter\sorter.db
Baseline copy:  %APPDATA%\com.richardnguyen.spotifysorter\backups\sorter-2026-10-19-baseline.db
                (state before real use: playlists, analysis, no suggestion log yet)

Tables to read:
  suggestion_log     one row per staged song: the 9 Suggested vibes shown (best first), the
                     playlists picked, whether audio analysis was available, commit/revert,
                     and (from 2026-10-19) `ranking`: every playlist's blended score with its
                     sound-alike share and artist-history score, and `settings`: the history
                     weight plus the analysis settings (audio/lyrics model, genre and lyrics
                     weights, neighbours) and when scores were computed.
  explore_log        songs added/removed/used to create a playlist from Explore: which tool,
                     what was searched, which section (sound / artist / misfit), and position.
  playlist_removals  songs that disappeared from a playlist (pruned in Spotify, found by sync,
                     or removed via Misfits), with when they had been added.
  filings            each committed song: playlists it went to, whether it was unliked, when
                     it was liked and filed, and whether the commit was reverted.


WHAT TO LOOK AT
---------------
1. Real-use accuracy of the Suggested vibes row
   - Committed, not reverted songs where a vibe playlist was picked: how often a pick was
     the top suggestion, in the top 3, among the 9. Compare with the offline test
     (top pick ~43%, top 3 ~68%, among 9 ~86%).
   - Split by: artist already in my playlists vs new artist; audio analysed vs not;
     month (does it drift?); Positive vs Negative mood playlists.
   - Where the pick ranked when it missed the 9 (from `ranking`): near misses (10-12)
     suggest showing more chips; far misses point at what the models can't see.

2. Which playlists get mixed up
   - For misses: which playlist was suggested first vs picked. Known offline: Positive vs
     Negative mood pairs (indie -> my heart oof, my heart oof -> my heart oop, sfl karaoke
     -> my heart oop). Check whether the same pairs dominate in real use.
   - Playlists that are almost never suggested correctly: candidates for a written
     "what defines this playlist" or for accepting that the app can't help there.

3. Replay ideas on real decisions (no new data needed)
   - Using `ranking` (sound and history kept separately): would a different history
     weight, or more chips, have caught more picks? Only change settings if the gain is
     clear (paired bootstrap, as in the offline tests).
   - If settings changed during the 3 months, compare periods using `settings`.

4. Explore in real use
   - From explore_log: which tool gets used, how often songs come from the sound section
     vs the artist section, and from what positions (are picks near the top? would a
     shorter list do?).
   - Offline reference: Grow found ~18% of hidden songs by artist + ~8.5% by sound;
     a few seeds ~14% of sound-alikes belonged to the seeds' playlist.

5. Misfits vs real pruning
   - For each pruned song (playlist_removals), where would Misfits have ranked it in that
     playlist? Offline: songs from a random other vibe ranked in the worst ~17%, songs
     from the most similar vibe barely below average (~44%). Real pruning is the honest
     test that wasn't possible before.

6. Habits worth checking
   - Songs unliked without filing anywhere; songs filed to many playlists at once.
   - Whether semester / Gold filing ever needed correcting (reverts).
   - API use per sync (quota trouble since the 2026-10-17 block?).


IDEAS ALREADY TESTED AND REJECTED (don't redo without new data)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Audio: CLAP, CLAP+MERT, MERT-330M, MuQ (none beat MERT-95M layer 4).
Lyrics models: bge-m3, e5-large. Qwen labels/chat. Release year, recency, learned metric,
pool-based mood check, typicality/anchor songs, per-playlist "sound/lyrics" tags
(lyrics-heavy weighting hurt top 3).
